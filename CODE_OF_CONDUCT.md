# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MeTTafy is intended to be an open, rigorous, welcoming research and engineering community. Contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and participants are expected to engage respectfully across differences in background, experience, discipline, implementation preference, and scientific opinion.

We value criticism of ideas, implementations, evidence, and claims. We do not accept harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, or sustained disruption of collaborative work.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- critique claims and code rather than people;
- distinguish evidence from interpretation;
- give credit for upstream work and correct attribution mistakes promptly;
- accept good-faith correction without retaliation;
- make space for newcomers to ask basic questions;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest when they materially affect a contribution or review;
- respect upstream communities and their licensing, terminology, and project boundaries.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project-controlled communication spaces, and public interactions made while representing MeTTafy.

## Reporting and enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for moderation and enforcement. If a concern can be raised safely in public, use the relevant issue or pull request. For sensitive conduct reports, contact the maintainer through an available private GitHub contact channel. If no private channel is available, open a minimal issue requesting confidential contact **without including sensitive incident details**.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close or lock discussions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude participants when necessary to protect the community. Reports will be handled with attention to privacy, proportionality, and fairness.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1, and by common open-source community moderation practice. It is intentionally shorter and tailored to this project's research context.
