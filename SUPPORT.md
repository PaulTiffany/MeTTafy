# Support

MeTTafy is an early research project maintained on a best-effort basis.

## Where to ask

- **Bug or reproducible incorrect behavior:** open a GitHub issue with a minimal fixture.
- **Feature or strategy-recognizer proposal:** open an issue describing the semantic strategy, evidence available in source programs, and how success could be verified.
- **Attribution or licensing correction:** open an issue or pull request; these are treated as high-priority project-health fixes.
- **Security concern:** follow `SECURITY.md` and do not post sensitive exploit details publicly.
- **General MeTTa/Hyperon questions:** prefer the relevant upstream community when the question is not specific to MeTTafy.

## What maintainers can reasonably support

The project currently targets the documented Python versions and the current `main` branch. Pre-alpha interfaces, Strategy IR shapes, and emitted MeTTa may change without compatibility guarantees.

Please include enough information for another person to reproduce the behavior. Small examples are strongly preferred over private application code.
